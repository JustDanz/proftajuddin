Ejaan Latin:

276. Yuta mantuk Sri Narendra, tanna suwe wus prapteng dalem puri, hangundangan patih rawuh, tumulih hatatanya, sapa si wong istri huni teka, wawu cahyane murub lir surya, hangungkuli garwa mami.

Arti Indonesia:

276. Kemudian pulang Sri Baginda, tak lama sampai di istana, memanggil Patihnya datang, lalu beliau bertanya, siapa wanita baru datang itu, sinar tubuhnya bagai surya mengalahkan semua istriku.

Arti Inggris:

276. Then he returned home, His Majesty, shortly arrived at the palace, called his Patih to come, then he asked, who was the new woman who came, the light of his body like the sun beat all my wives.
